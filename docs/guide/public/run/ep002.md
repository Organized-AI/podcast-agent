# AI in Healthcare: Hype or Reality?

**Episode** ep002 · **Host** David Chen · **Guests** Dr. Priya Patel, Alex Moore · **Length** ~05:10 (233 words)

## Summary

In "AI in Healthcare: Hype or Reality?", David Chen talks with Dr. Priya Patel, Alex Moore about healthcare, hype, reality and health. Dr. Priya Patel opens by noting: “Excited to discuss the real applications and limitations.” Alex Moore opens by noting: “Let’s be practical and look at use cases that are live today.” Dr. Patel argues: “Some hospitals use AI for reading X-rays or flagging urgent cases. But it’s assistive, not replacing clinicians.” Moore argues: “The regulatory barriers are huge. The FDA reviews these systems carefully, so full autonomy isn’t happening anytime soon.” Dr. Patel points to: “Smart triage chatbots on hospital websites or apps, helping patients figure out next steps before seeing a doctor.” Moore acknowledges: “Data privacy and bias are major hurdles. AI learns from existing clinical data, which may be skewed.” Dr. Patel observes: “Efficiency and clinician support. Hospitals want faster reads and fewer errors.” Moore observes: “The pandemic accelerated digital health adoption, including AI tools.” Dr. Patel looks ahead: “AI copilots that suggest treatment plans, but a human will always make the final call.” Moore looks ahead: “Interoperability is key. AI will work best when systems talk to each other securely.” David Chen concludes: “AI is powerful, but cautious optimism is the way forward.”
