# AI in Healthcare: Hype or Reality?

**Episode** ep002 · **Host** David Chen · **Guests** Dr. Priya Patel, Alex Moore · **Length** ~05:10 (233 words)

## Summary

On this episode of Tech Health Today, host David Chen opens by saying AI is everywhere but that in healthcare it is complicated, and that the show will separate hype from reality. Dr. Priya Patel says she is excited to discuss the real applications and limitations, while Alex Moore says he wants to be practical and look at use cases that are live today. David asks Priya whether diagnostics and triage assistants, which have been touted as the next big thing, are actually in use. Priya answers yes: some hospitals use AI for reading X-rays or flagging urgent cases, but it is assistive and does not replace clinicians. Alex adds that the regulatory barriers are huge, that the FDA reviews these systems carefully, and that full autonomy is not happening anytime soon. Asked by David for a concrete example patients might see, Priya points to smart triage chatbots on hospital websites or apps, which help patients figure out next steps before seeing a doctor. Alex then names data privacy and bias as major hurdles, explaining that AI learns from existing clinical data, which may be skewed. David asks why AI adoption continues anyway. Priya cites efficiency and clinician support, saying hospitals want faster reads and fewer errors. Alex adds that the pandemic accelerated digital health adoption, including AI tools. Looking ahead, David asks whether predictive diagnostics are next. Priya says yes, describing AI copilots that suggest treatment plans, and states that a human will always make the final call. Alex says interoperability is key and that AI will work best when systems talk to each other securely. In closing, David thanks Priya, Alex and the listeners, and says AI is powerful but cautious optimism is the way forward.
