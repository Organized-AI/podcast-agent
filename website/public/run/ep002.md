# AI in Healthcare: Hype or Reality?

**Episode** ep002 · **Host** David Chen · **Guests** Dr. Priya Patel, Alex Moore · **Length** ~05:10 (233 words)

## Summary

In this episode of Tech Health Today, host David Chen sits down with Dr. Priya Patel and Alex Moore to separate hype from reality in healthcare AI. David opens by noting that AI is everywhere but that its role in healthcare is complicated, while Priya promises a look at real applications and limitations and Alex urges a practical focus on use cases that are live today.
The conversation begins with diagnostics and triage assistants. Priya confirms that some hospitals already use AI to read X-rays and flag urgent cases, stressing that the technology is assistive and does not replace clinicians. Alex adds that regulatory barriers are huge, arguing that because the FDA reviews these systems carefully, full autonomy is not happening anytime soon.
Asked for a concrete example patients might encounter, Priya points to smart triage chatbots on hospital websites and apps, which help people work out their next steps before they see a doctor. Alex then turns to challenges, identifying data privacy and bias as major hurdles, since AI learns from existing clinical data that may be skewed.
David asks why adoption continues despite these issues. Priya cites efficiency and clinician support, noting that hospitals want faster reads and fewer errors. Alex says the pandemic accelerated digital health adoption, including AI tools.
Looking ahead, Priya anticipates AI copilots that suggest treatment plans, but maintains that a human will always make the final call. Alex says interoperability is key, with AI working best when systems talk to each other securely. David closes by calling AI powerful and cautious optimism the way forward.
